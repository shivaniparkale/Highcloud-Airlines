# Highcloud-Airlines
This project involves end-to-end business data analysis and dashboard development using Power BI. The objective was to transform raw business data into interactive visual reports that help monitor performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

##**Project Overview**

The dataset was cleaned, transformed, and analyzed to uncover meaningful business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, and operational efficiency. Interactive dashboards were created to provide a clear and dynamic view of key business metrics.

##**Objectives**
- Performed data cleaning and preprocessing to improve data quality
- Built interactive Power BI dashboards for business reporting
- Created KPI cards to track important business metrics
- Used charts and visualizations to analyze sales trends and performance
- Implemented slicers and filters for dynamic report interaction
- Generated insights to support strategic business decisions

##**Tools & Technologies Used**
- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

##**Visualizations Used**
- KPI Cards
- Bar Charts
- Line Charts
- Pie Charts
- Tables & Matrix
- Slicers and Filters

##**Business Insights Generated**
- Identified top-performing products and categories
- Analyzed monthly sales trends and revenue patterns
- Evaluated customer purchasing behavior
- Monitored overall business performance using KPIs
- Improved reporting efficiency through interactive dashboards

##**Outcome**
Successfully developed a professional Power BI dashboard that converts raw business data into meaningful insights, enabling better business analysis and decision-making.
